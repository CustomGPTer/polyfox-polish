1.0.0 26 November 2026

The first version of PolyFox Polish, a Polish course of its own, made to the same standard as PolyFox French.

  • The whole course, A1 to C2: 180 units, 2,160 words and about 2,880 sentences, every one spoken by a man’s or a woman’s voice.
  • Unit 1 is sounds and letters: how Polish is spelt and said, before the first words.
  • The cases come in one at a time, and the Word endings book shows all 1,373 nouns and adjectives of the course in every case, with what each case is for.
  • Verbs are taught in aspect pairs. Verb tables for 369 verbs: present, past (a man’s and a woman’s forms), future, conditional and imperative.
  • Ty first, then Pan and Pani. Lines about yourself are taught in your own form, and both forms are always accepted.
  • Typing is fair: free word order is accepted where Polish allows it, ja, ty and on may be left out or put in, and numbers can be typed as digits. A row of Polish letter keys sits above the answer box; up to A2 a missing Polish letter is pointed out and still passes, from B1 it counts.
  • A wrong answer says why: the wrong ending and the case it should be, the genitive after nie, a missing nie, or a Polish letter. Tap any word for its meaning and which form it is.
  • Speaking is checked on the phone by Whisper Small, which knows Polish sounds. A different number or a missing nie does not pass. Nothing is sent anywhere, and you can still mark yourself.
  • Twelve conversations, from meeting someone to a job interview, and 42 stories set in Poland, read aloud, six of them for beginners.
  • A grammar book of all 378 notes, reviews timed by FSRS, the method behind Anki, dictation, level exams, up to five people and a backup you keep.
